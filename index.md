      <p>
        I am a cosmologist working at the intersection of observational
        cosmology, statistical inference, and machine learning.
        My research focuses on testing cosmological models using
        observational probes of the expansion history of the Universe.
      </p>

      <p>
        I am particularly interested in dark energy and in data-driven
        approaches to cosmology, including Gaussian processes, symbolic
        regression, and machine-learning methods. I use observations such
        as Type Ia supernovae, baryon acoustic oscillations, cosmic
        chronometers, and galaxy ages to investigate the dynamics of the
        Universe.
      </p>

      <h2>Research Interests</h2>

<!-- ================= RESEARCH ================= -->

<section class="dark-section" id="research">

  <div class="section-container">

    <h2>Research</h2>

    <p>
      My research investigates the expansion history of the Universe
      through observational and data-driven approaches. A major focus
      is the study of dark energy using current cosmological surveys,
      including baryon acoustic oscillation and Type Ia supernova data.
    </p>

    <p>
      I also develop applications of machine learning and symbolic
      regression for model-independent cosmological reconstruction,
      as well as methods involving cosmic ages and gravitational-wave
      standard sirens.
    </p>

  </div>

</section>
